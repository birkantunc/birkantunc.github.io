My artwork explores the intricate beauty of traditional Islamic arts through a dedicated practice of historic methods and materials. I build my compositions on organic handmade paper, utilizing natural mineral pigments, watercolor, and acrylic alongside luminous applications of yellow, white, and green gold.

My practice primarily encompasses Classical Tazhib and Classical Miniature painting, characterized by ultra-fine brushwork and layered detailing. I also specialize in delicate shading traditions such as Halkâr—which creates softness, flow, and layered luminosity—and the Negative ("Çift Tahrir") technique. Through the harmony of traditional materials and meticulous craftsmanship, each piece reflects spiritual balance, ornamental geometry, and intricate floral motifs.
