I am an artist and educator with roots in Türkiye, specializing in Islamic illumination (tazhib) and classical miniature painting. Born in Sivas, I spent over a decade in Konya developing my artistic practice. I hold BA and MA degrees in Traditional Turkish Arts from Selçuk University, where I taught pattern design.

Having received my traditional tezhip ijazah in 2014, I have dedicated nearly two decades to preserving and practicing this art form. Since relocating to Philadelphia in 2018, I have continued my work using classical techniques and authentic materials—handmade papers, hand-ground natural mineral pigments, and real gold—to explore the harmony of geometric and floral forms in Ottoman and Persian manuscript traditions.

I regularly exhibit internationally and teach illumination and miniature painting through university, cultural center, and studio workshops. My artwork has also been featured on television, on book covers, and in private collections worldwide.
